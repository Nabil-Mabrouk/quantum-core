# One Core, Infinite Verticals

The ultimate goal of Quantum Core is to reach **Total Domain Independence**. 

### How to Fork Quantum Core
Because of our modular architecture, launching a new engineering SaaS (e.g., QuantumSolar or QuantumHVAC) requires only three steps:

1.  **Define the Vocabulary**: Map the domain manifest (change "Tank" to "Inverter").
2.  **Implement the Solver**: Write a specific Python module for the new physics.
3.  **Inject the Knowledge**: Upload the domain-specific library and Markdown guides.

### The Role of Generative AI
By maintaining a generic core, our AI integration becomes a **Universal Technical Writer**. Since the AI understands the generic structure of "Nodes and Components," it can analyze a wastewater plant just as easily as an electrical grid, drafting technical proposals and compliance reports with surgical precision.

We aren't just building a tool for water; we are building the future of industrial configuration.
